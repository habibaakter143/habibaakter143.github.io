## Research Projects
### Macro-benthic Faunal Diversity and Carbon Storage in The Mud Flat Ecosystem of Cox's Bazar Coast (Master's Thesis)[cite: 1]
Assessed macrobenthic diversity and sediment organic carbon in intertidal mudflats along the Cox's Bazar coast. Analyzed sediment texture and ecological indices to link benthic community structure with carbon storage[cite: 1].

### Strategic Site Selection: Applying the Weighted Overlay Method for Special Economic Zones in Chittagong District (Undergraduate Research Project)[cite: 1]
Applied the Weighted Overlay Method in ArcMap for site selection of Special Economic Zones (SEZs). Supported spatial decision-making using transportation accessibility, utilities, and land suitability criteria[cite: 1].

### Annual Chlorophyll-a Concentration and Sea Surface Temperature (SST) Analysis of The Bay of Bengal Using Satellite (Mini Project)[cite: 1]
Conducted satellite-based analysis of annual Chlorophyll-a and Sea Surface Temperature variability in the Bay of Bengal using Level-3 ocean colour data. Processed, reconstructed, and mapped patterns in SNAP and ArcGIS to assess spatial variability and oceanographic processes[cite: 1].
